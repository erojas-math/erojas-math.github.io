Master's thesis

Maximal Operators and the Fefferman-Stein Inequality on Bounded Domains

Edgar Rojas

California State Polytechnic University, Pomona

This thesis develops the theory of the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function and its applications in Sobolev spaces before studying restricted sharp maximal functions on irregular domains. It shows that the classical unweighted local Fefferman-Stein inequality fails on a cuspidal domain through its implication of an improved Poincare inequality, which fails on non-John domains. A weaker weighted inequality is then proved using a decomposition of the domain and localized estimates.

Committee: Fernando López-García (chair), John Rock, and Ignacio Ojea.
