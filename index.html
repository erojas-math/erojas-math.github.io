About me and interests

I am a mathematics instructor and prospective PhD student interested in analysis, particularly measure theory, functional analysis, maximal operators, Fourier analysis, Sobolev spaces, and fractal geometry.

Through my master's dissertation, I have done research on the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function, the restricted sharp maximal function, and local Fefferman-Stein inequalities. I am interested in how the geometry of a domain affects the validity and form of these inequalities.

Current projects

  • Find a function that exemplifies the invalidity of the unweighted Fefferman-Stein inequality on the cuspidal domain.
  • Prove that the the Hardy-Littlewood maximal on the characteristic function over convex domains in two dimensions returns interior angles. See example 1.1.5 in my dissertation.
  • I am currently writing solutions to problems on textbooks I am reading. I call this "Some Mathematics for You".

Master's thesis

Maximal Operators and the Fefferman-Stein Inequality on Bounded Domains

The classical unweighted local inequality fails on a cuspidal domain. The thesis then establishes a weaker weighted version through a decomposition of the domain and a local-to-global argument.
